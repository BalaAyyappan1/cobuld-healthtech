Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2026

At Coblud Healthtech, we use cookies to enhance your experience and provide personalized healthcare-related services. These tools allow us to efficiently manage information flow between blood banks, hospitals, and potential donors.

1. Essential Cookies

These are necessary for the technical operation of our platform. They enable core features such as secure logins for medical institutions and donor portal access.

2. Analytics Cookies

We use these to analyze website traffic and user interactions. This data helps us optimize the coordination between donors and hospitals to improve emergency response efficiency.

3. Functional Cookies

These cookies allow our platform to remember your preferences and provide a more personalized experience, ensuring that relevant health alerts and location-based donor data are prioritized.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact the functionality of critical parts of the Coblud Healthtech ecosystem.

5. Contact Us

For questions regarding our use of cookies or data handling, please reach out to our privacy team at support@cobludhealthtech.com .
