Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

At Coblud Healthtech, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information across our ecosystem.

1. Information We Collect

  • Blood Banks and Hospitals: We collect and store inventory data regarding available blood supplies and institutional contact details.
  • Donors: We gather personal data including name, contact information, blood type, and donation history to facilitate life-saving connections.
  • Users Seeking Blood: When searching for donors, we collect relevant data to provide the most appropriate and urgent matches.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information from blood banks, hospitals, and registered donors to facilitate blood donations to those in need.

Additionally, we may use anonymized data to analyze usage patterns, improve our matching algorithms, and enhance our overall service offerings.

3. Data Sharing and Security

Third Parties: We share necessary data with hospitals and blood banks to fulfill donation requests. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

Data Protection: We implement robust technical measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse within the Coblud Healthtech network.

4. Your Rights & Updates

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your data. This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our services; any significant changes will be communicated via our platform.

5. Contact Us

For more information or to exercise your rights, please contact our support team at: support@cobludhealthtech.com
